## UI walkthroughs

Both language flows used separate disposable fixture directories, with three completed child histories and one media-byte fixture each. Confirmation, 4/4 completion, original-path reads, and restoration were exercised. All four source files were regular files again after each run, with independently equal source/target hashes; prefixes are in [WALKTHROUGH.md](../launch/WALKTHROUGH.md).

The fixture simulates mount identity and drive capacity; transactions use real temporary bytes. An attempted second docking into a retained fixture destination stopped safely without overwriting; the English walkthrough used a fresh fixture. The videos edit observed UI screenshots rather than continuously recording the operations. Neither this video nor the physical byte trial establishes Codex restart/remount, power-loss recovery, all metadata preservation, every drive's compatibility, or real-world speed.
